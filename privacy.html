Privacy

How we handle your information.

This page explains how we handle information on the FUSED ID website. If you use a FUSED service, we agree its data access, providers and retention with you separately.

Updated 11 November 2026

About this website

This website is operated by FUSED ID Pty Ltd. Its enquiry form sends the details you enter to FUSED when you choose Send enquiry. We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site.

The company that hosts the website may keep ordinary technical request logs, which can include an IP address, browser or device information, the requested page and time. Those logs are controlled by the relevant hosting arrangements and may be used for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

Website analytics and cookies

Google Analytics helps us understand which pages are useful, where visits come from and whether an enquiry was successfully received. It can use cookies and browser identifiers to measure visits, interactions and general device information. Google processes this information and may process it outside Australia.

Our enquiry tracking records a successful submission without passing your name, business name, email address, phone number or message to Google Analytics. Google Signals and advertising personalisation are disabled in our website configuration.

We do not load analytics when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control preference. You can also manage cookies in your browser or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services for more detail.

The workspace preview

The customer-view preview shows fictional information. Switching tabs and exploring a sample learning review do not connect to a customer account or live AI service. The sample download creates a text file on your device; copying a sample prompt uses your device clipboard. No workspace input is sent to FUSED.

The optional role brief

Text entered in the role worksheet is not submitted or saved by this website. The copy button writes the draft to your device clipboard only when you choose it; if copying is unavailable, it displays text you can copy yourself. Printing or saving a PDF uses your browser. Share the resulting draft only when you are ready.

When you contact FUSED

When enabled on the enquiry form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated submissions using browser and connection information. Cloudflare processes that information to perform the security check and may process it outside Australia. The check is separate from Google Analytics. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for more detail.

If you use the enquiry form, we receive your name, business name, email address, optional phone number and message. If you contact us by phone, we use the information you choose to provide to handle your enquiry. We use it to respond, understand the possible engagement, prepare or administer an agreed service and meet legitimate business, security and legal needs.

Please do not send sensitive, confidential or production information in a first enquiry. We can agree an appropriate channel and handling arrangements if detailed material is needed.

Service data is agreed for the role

An AI colleague may need access to business information, accounts or systems. The relevant sources, permissions, technology providers, data locations, retention, monitoring, incident paths and responsibilities are identified during design and documented for the engagement. Access should be limited to what the agreed role and skills need.

The intended system of record and the return path for completed work are also agreed. Business systems remain authoritative where the design depends on them; any processing or storage elsewhere is made explicit for the engagement.

Your business knowledge remains specific to your business. FUSED may reuse its general methods, patterns and know-how, but does not treat one organisation's confidential knowledge as source material for another.

Sharing, storage and protection

Information may be handled by service providers used for email, hosting, business administration or the agreed technical service. The providers and regions relevant to an operational engagement are confirmed as part of that scope. We do not sell personal information.

FUSED uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and service. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so the design also includes practical limits, monitoring and escalation where relevant.

Retention and your questions

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose, an agreed engagement, security, record-keeping or applicable obligations. Retention for operational service data is agreed for the engagement.

To ask what information FUSED holds about you, request a correction or raise a privacy concern, use our contact form or call 03 8669 1414. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Scope of this notice: privacy obligations can depend on the organisation, information, role and jurisdiction involved. This page does not replace engagement-specific contractual or compliance requirements.
