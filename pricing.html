The practical details

A clear offer.
A clear understanding.

We work through the role, access, operating model and running costs before you commit. Your proposal records what is included.

Why is the licence and deployment price “from $12,000”?

It is a starting price, not a fixed fee for any project. A single standard role needs a different amount of work from a bespoke workflow with data migration, multiple integrations or complex review rules. We quote the software licence, implementation, training and agreed handover for your requirements before you commit. Recurring provider costs and optional support are identified separately.

Does a perpetual licence mean no ongoing costs?

It means no ongoing FUSED subscription is required to run the licensed version. You still pay for infrastructure, AI/token usage and any third-party licences, and provide the people to operate it. Future software updates and optional support or management are purchased separately.

What does unlimited mean in the perpetual licence?

There is no FUSED licence limit on users, AI colleagues or API connections within the agreed organisation. We configure the first colleague to the agreed scope. Your team configures further roles and plugins, or commissions that work separately. Infrastructure capacity, AI usage, third-party terms and permissions still apply.

What exactly does the perpetual licence cover?

You can keep running the delivered workplace-software version in the supported deployment agreed with us. FUSED retains its software IP; source code and third-party licences are not transferred. Future software updates are separate purchases. Continued compatibility with changing external services may require updates or maintenance.

Does customer hosting keep all AI processing on our premises?

Not necessarily. The workplace runs on your agreed infrastructure, but cloud AI providers may process requests outside it. We confirm the models, providers, regions and data flows before deployment.

What is the monthly subscription paying for?

Ongoing responsibility for the agreed service: checking that work completes, investigating failures, maintaining connections and updating knowledge and skills as the business changes. For example, we check an affected workflow when a connected system changes, and test an updated instruction before release. Hosting, monitoring and technical assistance support that work.

Can you build something our team can run without a FUSED subscription?

Yes. We can scope a standalone workflow in your existing suitable AI tools, with editable prompts, customer-specific skills, worked examples and training. Your team runs and maintains it. This is separately quoted and does not include a FUSED workplace licence, ongoing learning service or automated integrations unless expressly scoped. See the workflow build and handover.

Can we use our existing ChatGPT or Claude subscription?

Yes, where your platform and plan support the agreed connection. Your AI plan or credits cover work performed within that platform. Tasks run by FUSED use your FUSED processing allowance. We explain both parts, permissions and any additional charges before you start.

What counts as an additional connection?

An agreed integration with another business system or external service, such as a CRM or accounting system. SharePoint, Teams, Outlook and supported AI-platform access are already included. We confirm what each connection can read or do, vendor requirements and any specialist work or third-party charges upfront.

What does a specialist add to a standard colleague?

A standard colleague starts with one focused workflow. A specialist brings developed methods and an agreed programme of work for its role. For example, the Web Growth Architect combines website analysis, customer questions and approved updates into ongoing site improvement.

The specialist proposal sets the outputs, workload, review frequency and improvement allowance. Its service fee is additional to workplace maintenance. Required third-party software licences and specialist integration work are identified separately.

Explore the specialist roles → or see connected tools.

Can we move from managed service to running it ourselves?

Yes, through a separately scoped perpetual licence and supported deployment. A managed subscription does not automatically include that licence or a free migration. We agree licence costs, transition work, export formats and handover before you move. Your business knowledge, outputs and agreed customer-specific work remain yours; FUSED’s managed operation and ongoing improvement end with the subscription.

You can also use your retained customer material with another suitable platform or provider. The licence is required if you want to keep running the FUSED workplace software itself.
