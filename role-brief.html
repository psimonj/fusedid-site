Cloud-colleague role brief

01What useful difference should the role make?

Describe what would make the work easier or more consistent for your team.

02Where is the work currently getting stuck?

Note the business area, recurring frustration or responsibility that keeps being squeezed out.

03What could be the first responsibility?

A broad area, such as helping sales, is enough. Leave this blank if you would rather work it out together.

04Where could it start learning?

List the people, website pages, documents, examples or systems that hold useful knowledge.

05How would your team work with the colleague?

Think about its name, reporting relationship and the familiar tools people would naturally use.

06What must remain a human decision?

Identify commitments, sensitive matters, unusual situations or risks that should always be handed to a person.
