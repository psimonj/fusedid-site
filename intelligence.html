A managed improvement cycle

Let good work
teach the next piece.

A correction becomes valuable when it changes what happens next. FUSED provides a managed cycle for turning evidence into useful improvement.

  1. 01 / CAPTURE

    Something is learned.

    An approved source, decision or reviewer correction provides new evidence.

  2. 02 / CHECK

    Keep what matters.

    Check the source, resolve conflicts and confirm what changes. Keep the reason.

  3. 03 / APPLY

    Use the understanding.

    Accepted knowledge informs your colleague’s work within its agreed role.

  4. 04 / IMPROVE

    Refine the method.

    Review results, test changes to working instructions and check the next output.

↶ Bring the next result back into the review. Learning is useful when it improves the work.

Keep the knowledge current.

A confirmed fact or decision can inform current work under the agreed rules. Its source, reason and history remain available.

Improve how the work is done.

Recurring reviews consider whether feedback should change a prompt, skill or workflow. Test the change, check the result and then release it through the agreed process.

REVIEWER FEEDBACK
“The example is too abstract. Show the process in an Australian workplace.”

A useful correction from a reviewed draft.

A PROPOSED SKILL UPDATE

Make the method more specific.

For each process explanation, include a relevant Australian workplace example. Check that the example demonstrates the steps and uses approved terminology.

Before release: test the revised instructions on representative work and have the reviewer check the result.

The next check: Does the revised draft include a relevant example, meet the requirement and need less correction? Keep the evidence, including results that show the change did not help.

New information is not automatically a new rule. A review may find that no change is needed. The review schedule and responsibilities are agreed for your service.

Your questions.

Can we run FUSED Intelligence ourselves?
Yes. A separately quoted perpetual workplace-software licence lets you run FUSED on your own supported infrastructure, with unlimited users within the agreed organisation and no FUSED subscription. Your team handles operation, knowledge upkeep, connections and improvement. Future software updates are purchased separately; optional management is available under an agreed service scope. See the licence and responsibilities.
Can we still take Discovery and build independently?
Yes. A separate Comprehensive Discovery & Design engagement gives you an editable workflow design, requirements and implementation plan. Your team or another provider can build from it. Working prompts, skills, examples and training are delivered through a separately quoted pilot. Neither stage commits you to a FUSED subscription.
Who owns the knowledge and instructions?
Your business keeps its knowledge, outputs, tailored workflows and customer-specific skills, including agreed improvements. We confirm export formats and handover arrangements at the start. FUSED retains its software IP and reusable methods. When a managed subscription ends, its operation, maintained connections and ongoing improvement end too. You retain your agreed material. Running the FUSED software independently requires the separate perpetual licence and a supported deployment; licence and transition work are quoted upfront.
