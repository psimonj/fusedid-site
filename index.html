Before we talk

A few
good questions.

Want to talk it through?
sales@fusedid.com

Can this help if we already use AI?
Yes. ChatGPT and Claude are useful productivity tools. FUSED adds the maintained workplace: shared business knowledge, tested skills, connected tools and agreed responsibilities. You can access FUSED through an agreed connection to a supported AI platform, or work with your colleague through email and Teams. See how the connection works.
What do we actually get first?
An agreed result you can test: a reply and handover, a review-ready draft or another useful output. We define the workflow, prepare the knowledge and skills, and test representative work with your people. Choose a standalone workflow in your own tools, a licensed workplace or a managed service. Each scope and fee is agreed before building.
Do we need to know exactly what we want it to do?
No. A broad role or a part of the business that needs more capacity is enough to begin. We help shape a useful starting point. The colleague can also suggest skills as it learns where your team needs help.
Can we run the workplace ourselves?
Yes. Perpetual licence and deployment starts from $12,000 AUD ex GST, with your first colleague configured to the agreed scope. Run the delivered software on your supported servers and accounts, with no FUSED subscription. The licence allows unlimited users, AI colleagues and API connections within your organisation; configuration work and provider usage are separate. We agree the documentation and training your team needs for handover. Future software updates and optional support are separate. Explore the perpetual licence.
Can we use the work without an ongoing FUSED service?
Yes. A separately scoped workflow build can give your team editable prompts, customer-specific skills, worked examples and training to use in your own suitable AI tools. You run and maintain it, without a FUSED subscription. A perpetual licence is needed only if you also want to run the FUSED workplace software. See the independent workflow option.
Does everything have to run in Microsoft 365?
No. Outlook, Teams and SharePoint are included connection options in the paid subscription. An agreed connection lets you work through a supported ChatGPT, Claude or other AI platform. We confirm plan compatibility, permissions and where information is processed before connecting it. Explore your tools and connections.
