When Microsoft Is Right — And When It Is Not

FUSED ID is technology-agnostic, not technology-blind. Microsoft is often a strong foundation because it is familiar, widely supported and already embedded inside many businesses.

But we do not force Microsoft where it is not the right answer. The objective is business performance, not platform loyalty.

Microsoft is usually a good fit when:

  • Your team already uses Microsoft 365
  • Your documents live in SharePoint or OneDrive
  • Your communication runs through Teams or Outlook
  • You need low-code workflows and approvals
  • You want dashboards, governance and visibility

We may recommend another path when:

  • Your core system already handles the workflow well
  • A specialist platform is clearly better suited
  • The cost or complexity is not justified
  • Your team needs a lighter deployment first
  • Microsoft would add more friction than value
