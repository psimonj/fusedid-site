A quality workflow in practice

From reported issue to reviewed closure.

The colleague can organise the process and keep it moving. The people responsible for quality still judge causes, risks and closure.

01 / CAPTURE

Record the issue

Structure what happened, when, where and what initial evidence exists.

02 / ORGANISE

Assemble evidence

Link relevant records, documents, observations and previous events.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Support the analysis

Prepare timelines, patterns and questions while a responsible person judges cause.

04 / ACT

Coordinate actions

Maintain owners, dates, dependencies, reminders and supporting evidence.

05 / REVIEW

Confirm and improve

Present closure evidence, recurring patterns and proposed system improvements.

Trust earned through actual work

Trust is earned.
One responsibility at a time.

We agree what good work looks like and test ordinary requests, missing information and awkward cases. The checks reflect the consequences of getting that particular job wrong.

BEFORE MORE RESPONSIBILITY

Check the work.

Check accuracy, source use, corrections and handovers across representative work. A convincing answer or time spent in training is not enough on its own.

WHEN YOU AGREE

Agree what it may do.

Record the actions, sources, limits and decisions that still need a person. New skills and broader responsibilities require their own checks and approval.

AS THE WORK CONTINUES

Keep checking as things change.

Monitor results. If quality drops or something changes, pause affected actions or return them to review. Check the fix before agreeing to resume.

Learning more does not grant permission to do more. Updates to business knowledge do not change a skill’s authority. A skill can remain useful with a person reviewing every output; the review arrangements are agreed for your service.

Practical controls

Clear rules for everyday work.

Checks and approvals match the work, so routine actions can move while important decisions remain visible.

01 / DEFINE

Agree the role and rules

Clarify what the colleague is responsible for, what it may do, which information it may trust and who makes each decision.

  • Purpose and expected results
  • Approved information
  • Permissions
  • Decision owners
02 / WATCH

Keep useful records

Retain enough information to find problems and explain important outcomes.

  • Activity records
  • Unusual situations
  • Failed handovers
  • Review measures
03 / IMPROVE

Fix and strengthen

Find what is causing recurring problems. Agree changes to the skill, then check that the fix worked.

  • Cause analysis
  • Corrective actions
  • Change approval
  • Check that the fix worked
