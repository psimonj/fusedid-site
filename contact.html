Before you contact us

Straight answers.

Can this help if we already use AI?
Yes. FUSED can build on suitable tools you already use. The difference is a colleague with ongoing business understanding and a team looking after its learning, skills and support.
Do I need an AI strategy first?
No. A broad business role or a recurring frustration is enough to start the conversation. We help you choose a useful first role. The technical approach follows from the work and the way your people operate.
What if we do not know the first skill yet?
That is fine. Use the starting service for questions, current guidance and keeping track of confirmed decisions. The colleague researches, asks and keeps learning as you work together. It can suggest skills, but you choose whether to add them. Each is scoped and quoted separately.
What happens after I email?
We talk through the opportunity and decide whether there is a sensible role to design. If there is, we explain what you would receive first, the input needed from your team, the support we provide and the fees before any build begins.
How will the engagement be charged?
There is an initial fee for setup and your colleague’s induction, then an ongoing service covering agreed learning, operation and support. Additional skills are quoted separately, including their ongoing fees. One skill can handle many related tasks. We explain relevant software or usage costs before you commit.
Can FUSED work with Microsoft 365?
Yes. Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Power Platform and Dynamics can be practical parts of the service where they fit. Identity, licensing, access and supported capabilities are confirmed against your environment. Microsoft is not mandatory.
Does this page collect my information?
There is no enquiry form or analytics script. The email link opens your own email application. Ordinary hosting logs may still be kept; see our privacy page.
