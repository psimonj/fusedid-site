Useful work you can recognise

Here’s what comes back.

Each task has an agreed result and clear approval rules. These examples show what your team could receive and which decisions remain yours.

Enquiries & replies01

Get an enquiry ready for the next conversation.

Check the product information, ask for missing details and prepare or send a reply under the rules you choose. Prices, delivery promises and unusual requests come back to the responsible person.

  • Website and inbox enquiries
  • Product answers with sources
  • Missing details followed up
  • Sales records and handovers
You receiveA reply, the useful details in one place and a clear next action for sales.
Marketing content02

Make time to tell people what you do.

Turn your product knowledge, project stories and useful advice into LinkedIn posts, website copy or regular updates. Your team reviews the claims, voice and images before publication.

  • Content ideas
  • LinkedIn and website drafts
  • Image suggestions
  • Revisions from your feedback
You receiveA draft in your business’s voice, with an image suggestion and any facts that need checking.
Sales research & follow-up03

Go into the conversation knowing who you are talking to.

Start with the kinds of businesses you want to work with, or examples of your best clients. Your colleague finds similar companies, checks the fit and prepares an approach. You choose who to contact and how.

  • Find businesses in your target markets
  • See which prospects to contact and why
  • Company research and outreach drafts
  • Agreed follow-up and record updates
You receiveA prioritised prospect list with sources, reasons for fit and outreach drafts ready for review.
See an example workflow
Your request
“Find businesses like these clients in our target region. Show me why they’re worth a conversation.”
What NOVA does
Uses your examples and criteria to research companies, group them by fit and draft a relevant introduction. Your feedback helps refine the next search.
What comes back
A list showing each company’s market, location, source links, fit and suggested approach. Uncertain details are flagged.
You decide
The target market, exclusions and sending rules. Outreach, follow-up and system connections are agreed before they are added.

Example workflow. We agree the sources, scope and approvals with you.

Operations & administration04

Pull the job together before it lands on someone’s desk.

Collect the information for a request, organise documents and chase what is missing. Update approved records and prepare draft invoices in a connected accounting system such as Xero. Your team sees what is ready and what still needs a decision.

  • Job packs and routine documents
  • Information requests
  • Registers, data entry and draft invoices
  • Regular progress summaries
You receiveAn organised job pack or report, with missing information and decisions clearly flagged.
Quality & improvement05

Keep the improvement moving after the meeting ends.

Report an issue or improvement idea in everyday language. Your colleague asks for missing details, helps investigate and follows agreed actions through to evidence and review. Recurring issues and useful improvements become easier to see.

  • Issue capture and investigation questions
  • Action owners, dates and evidence
  • Recurring issues and improvement reports
  • Procedure drafts and owner approvals
You receiveAn improvement record with owners, progress and evidence, plus a summary of patterns and results for management.
See an example workflow
Your request
“This order issue has happened again. Help us understand why and follow the fix through.”
What NOVA does
Gathers the facts, links related issues and asks questions to support the investigation. It follows up agreed owners and dates, collects evidence and flags actions still open.
What comes back
An action record and management summary: what changed, what remains open and whether the issue is recurring. Confirmed improvements are recorded; estimated benefits stay separate from measured results.
You decide
Your team confirms the cause, approves changes and checks that the fix worked before closure. Document updates can be included, with owner approval, version history and notifications.

Example workflow. We agree the register, reporting and document connections with you.

Recruitment support06

Spend more time understanding the applicants.

Compare applications with the actual role, ask agreed follow-up questions and bring the answers together. Your colleague also answers applicants’ questions from role information you have approved. Your team handles interviews and hiring decisions.

  • Evidence against role requirements
  • Missing details clarified with applicants
  • Answers from approved role information
  • Comparable summaries and interview questions
You receiveA review pack with evidence against your criteria, applicants’ answers and useful questions for each interview.
See an example workflow
Your request
“Help us review these applications for the service coordinator role. Clarify their relevant experience before we meet.”
What NOVA does
Compares the supplied evidence with agreed job requirements, asks applicants the approved preliminary questions and collects their responses. Questions about the role are answered from your approved information or passed to your team.
What comes back
A consistent brief for each applicant, linking the evidence and answers to your criteria, with remaining questions clearly marked.
You decide
Your team chooses the criteria and questions, reviews the evidence and decides who progresses to an interview or an offer.

Example workflow. We agree applicant communications, access and information handling before use.

  • Wider document libraries

  • Access by role
  • Source and version checks
You receiveAnswers from a wider set of approved sources, with references and conflicting guidance flagged.

Learning content & expert review

From approved sources
to a stronger first draft.

A content colleague can help a specialist team prepare and update learning resources without losing the judgement that makes them useful.

Use approved source material, current requirements, style guidance and templates to prepare draft guides, assessment material and requirement mapping. Flag gaps, conflicts and unsupported statements for the reviewer.

Capture corrections as evidence for the next improvement review. Check whether the finished work takes less effort—not just whether the first draft appears faster.

A standalone build and handover lets your team use this method in its own suitable AI tools, without an ongoing FUSED subscription.

Skill, task or improvement?

One skill. Many tasks.

You agree what each skill covers before we build it. A recurring task uses the same skill; workload and any usage costs are covered by the service terms.

SKILL

For example, handle and qualify inbound enquiries across agreed channels, using approved knowledge and a defined handover.

Defined within your service scope
TASK

For example, answer this product question, follow up this enquiry or update the agreed record after the conversation.

Within agreed workload and usage terms
IMPROVEMENT

Making the work better

For example, tighten a question, add an approved answer or improve a handover based on actual use and evidence.
