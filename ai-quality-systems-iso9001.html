Ongoing knowledge management

A one-off upload
is only the beginning.

Decisions change. Exceptions recur. Better ways of working emerge. Your colleague helps capture that knowledge, check its meaning and keep the right people informed through email and Teams.

ISO 9001 principles sit at the centre of FUSED’s method: clear responsibilities, controlled information and continual improvement. Your approved records remain the source of truth. The colleague helps maintain and use them, within the access and update rules you agree.

Useful history

With a document-update skill

Turn an agreed change
into current guidance.

Your colleague can prepare standard operating procedure (SOP) updates and carry approved revisions into connected documents. FUSED builds the workflow around your owners, approvals, access and records.

For example: NOVA learns that delivery dates need operations approval, then notices an older procedure still promising next-day dispatch. It flags the conflict and can prepare a correction for the document owner to approve.

01 / CAPTURE

Understand the decision

Record what changed, why, who authorised it and when it applies. Clarify whether it is an exception or a new rule.

02 / REVIEW

Check the impact

Identify affected procedures, people and work. Flag conflicts, missing controls or possible nonconformities for the responsible owner to review.

03 / UPDATE

Publish with approval

Draft revisions, obtain the required approval and publish on the agreed date. Notify authorised people and retain the earlier versions and approval history.

04 / IMPROVE

Check the result

Follow the agreed actions and review recurring exceptions. Bring evidence of what improved, or what still needs attention, back to the owner.
