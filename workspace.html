The method behind the task.

Customer-specific working instructions for NOVA’s enquiry role.

Enquiry response / specialist skill

Check the fit before suggesting a product.

  1. Understand the application. Ask where and how the product will be used. Keep missing requirements visible.
  2. Use the current guidance. Match the requirement to approved product information and retain the source.
  3. Prepare a useful reply. Answer supported questions and include an enquiry summary for sales.
  4. Keep commitments with their owner. Ask Operations about an unconfirmed date. Sales confirms price and terms.
  5. Check before handover. Include the application, quantity, relevant drawing and unresolved questions.

Sample customer-specific instructions. The guidance your team can inspect and the way it requests changes are agreed for your service. FUSED’s underlying platform and proprietary methods remain part of the managed service.

Enquiry skill / suggested improvementProposed change
“Ask for the application before recommending this range.”

Source: feedback on a reviewed enquiry · Owner: Sales team

Previous instruction

Find a product using the details supplied in the enquiry.

Proposed instruction

Confirm the application first. If it is missing, ask before recommending a product.

Check before release: try a complete enquiry and one with the application missing. Confirm that NOVA asks the right question and still handles a complete enquiry without unnecessary delay.

Example outcome: checked and applied.

The next incomplete enquiry prompts NOVA to ask for the application. The sales handover includes it once confirmed.

Keep the source, reason and previous guidance. Review whether the change reduces missing information in later work.
