FUSED Intelligence · Your shared business understanding

When the answer changes,
your AI needs to know.

A useful AI colleague needs to understand more than the question in front of it. It needs your products, processes, priorities and the reasons behind your decisions.

“We changed that last week.” “That was only a one-off.” “Who else needs to know?” Your colleague asks those questions, captures confirmed answers and stops relying on old guidance. It can also spot when the same exception keeps coming back.

FUSED manages the sources, questions and checks behind that learning. Your approved records remain the source of truth. A configured workflow can help update controlled documents once the responsible person approves.

Your team can review knowledge, contribute corrections and give feedback through agreed customer access. Permissions determine which changes can be made directly and which need review.

Find an answer

Understand a change

“Does this replace the old rule?” Check who approved the change, why it matters and when it takes effect.

Spot a pattern

“This exception keeps coming up.” Flag the pattern and ask whether the process needs improving.

Keep people informed

04 / Quality & continual improvement

The specialist service: follow an issue from its first report through investigation, actions and a check that the fix worked. Agreed workflows keep owners, evidence and controlled document changes together, ready for the people responsible to review.

  • Capture issues and ask questions that help investigate what happened.
  • Link related problems and follow owners, deadlines and missing evidence.
  • Prepare procedure revisions for the document owner to review.
  • Assemble quality review packs and gather evidence against agreed checks to help your team judge whether corrective actions worked.

Your team keeps quality authority. People confirm causes, assess risk, approve controlled changes and accept closure. The role supports your management system and does not provide certification.
