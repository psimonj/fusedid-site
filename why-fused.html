When you want it to take on more

Keep the service working. Make the work better.

When you want your colleague to send replies, update records or follow up missing details, we build skills around that work. Each skill has an agreed scope, approvals and support.

01 / AGREE

Define the work and who decides

Agree what starts the job, what the colleague should finish, which actions need approval and who receives the result. Include the follow-up.

02 / TEST

Test the whole job

Try ordinary requests and awkward ones from your business. Check the answer, its sources and what happens when information is missing or a connection fails.

03 / MANAGE

Keep it useful as things change

FUSED investigates problems and maintains the agreed service. Review the results and corrections together. You decide when a skill has earned more responsibility.

Each skill is scoped and quoted separately. We agree support arrangements and response expectations before launch.

ILLUSTRATIVE SERVICE REVIEW

The colleague keeps asking for the same missing detail.

Repeated questions can reveal a process worth improving.

  1. Notice the pattern. Enquiries repeatedly arrive without a drawing or required date.
  2. Improve the starting point. Agree a clearer enquiry prompt or intake template with the sales team.
  3. Update and check. FUSED updates the agreed workflow and reviews whether fewer requests need chasing.

Improvements within scope are managed as agreed; a new skill or substantial change is quoted separately.

Data, access & service trust

Agree what the colleague can see, do and retain.

Before we connect your colleague, we work through what it needs access to, what it can do and what it must ask you first.

01 / SCOPE

Relevant access only

Define the sources, accounts, channels and actions needed for the role. Permissions are limited to the agreed work and reviewed when the role changes.

02 / SOURCES

Knowledge you can check

You should be able to tell where an answer came from and what still needs checking. We agree who can confirm changes, how old information is replaced and what the colleague must not keep.

03 / EVIDENCE

See what happened

Keep a useful record of what the colleague did, who approved it and what went wrong, so problems can be investigated and fixed.

04 / PROVIDERS

Know where your data goes

Before we begin, know which providers handle your information, where it is stored, how long it is kept and who to contact if something goes wrong.

Before we begin, we work through your privacy, security and business requirements with you, including any rules your contracts or industry require you to follow.
