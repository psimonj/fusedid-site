Before you contact us

Straight answers.

Can this help if we already use AI?
Yes. Use FUSED through your existing ChatGPT or Claude subscription where supported, or work with your colleague through Outlook and Teams. FUSED supplies the maintained business knowledge, tested skills and agreed connections behind the work. See how the connection works.
Do I need an AI strategy first?
No. A broad business role or a recurring frustration is enough to start the conversation. We help you choose a useful first role. The technical approach follows from the work and the way your people operate.
What if we do not know where AI would help?
Start with the problem. Discovery & Design can identify opportunities, map the workflows and define what a useful result would be. You receive a plan you can build on, without committing to a managed service.
What happens after I send an enquiry?
We talk through the opportunity and decide whether there is a sensible role to design. If there is, we explain what you would receive first, the input needed from your team, the support we provide and the fees before any build begins.
Can we start with Discovery and build it ourselves?
Yes. A separate Comprehensive Discovery & Design engagement gives you an editable workflow design and implementation plan to take to your own team or provider. A separately quoted pilot turns that plan into tested prompts, skills and worked examples, with training to use them in your own suitable AI tools. Neither requires a FUSED subscription. You can also buy a perpetual software licence to run a FUSED workplace on your supported infrastructure. Compare the operating options.
How will the engagement be charged?
For a managed workplace, there is an initial setup fee and a subscription covering its agreed operation, connections and improvement. Self-managed licence and deployment starts from $12,000 AUD ex GST, with the final price quoted to scope and no FUSED subscription. You pay infrastructure and provider usage directly. Standalone workflows in your own AI tools are separately quoted. Future software updates and optional support are separate. See pricing and responsibilities. Starter Discovery & Design is included with workplace setup. Comprehensive discovery starts from $8,000 AUD ex GST, quoted based on scope; specialist integrations and additional work are scoped before you commit.
Can FUSED work with Microsoft 365?
Yes. SharePoint, Teams and Outlook connections are included in paid FUSED subscriptions, alongside access from supported AI platforms. Microsoft and other provider licences remain separate. We confirm permissions and supported capabilities with your team; other systems can also be connected.
Does this page collect my information?
When you submit the form, we receive the contact details and message you provide so we can respond. Google Analytics measures site use and confirmed enquiries without receiving the details you enter. When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated submissions. Ordinary hosting logs may also be kept; see our privacy page.
