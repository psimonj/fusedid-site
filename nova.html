How we would work together

“Loop me in. I’ll ask what I need to know.”

NOVA researches your products, market and competitors, learns from the conversations you include it in, and checks what is unclear. It remembers confirmed decisions and revisits its understanding as your business changes.

A USEFUL FIRST QUESTION“What do we normally tell someone asking for next-day delivery?”

NOVA finds the current guidance, explains where it came from and flags anything that needs confirming.

01 / WORKING STYLE

What working with NOVA could feel like

  • Short, useful answers with the detail available when you need it
  • Questions that uncover missing information
  • A clear distinction between what it knows and what it needs to check
  • Suggestions when the same question or problem keeps coming back
02 / GETTING TO KNOW YOU

What NOVA needs to learn

  • Your products, customers and place in the market
  • Why you recommend one option over another
  • Who can confirm prices, delivery dates and technical advice
  • What a good reply sounds like in your business

Your input still matters. NOVA cannot know private history it has never seen. It researches and asks; your team fills in the gaps and confirms important changes. FUSED manages the learning and checks its understanding with you.
