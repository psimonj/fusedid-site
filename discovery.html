A practical example

Updating a customer guide.
What would we design?

Your team currently finds the source documents, checks versions, writes the update and sends it for review. Discovery works out how to make that whole job easier.

The design brief

Produce a draft
the reviewer can check.

Inputs: the current guide, approved source documents and a list of required changes.

Output: a draft with source references, gaps and questions for the reviewer.

Human decision: the document owner approves the content and release.

Example workflow.

  1. CHECK THE STARTING MATERIALUse the right documents.

    Confirm versions and permissions. Ask for missing information before drafting.

  2. PREPARE THE WORKDraft from approved sources.

    Follow the agreed structure, link supporting evidence and flag anything the sources do not answer.

  3. MAKE REVIEW EASIERReturn the draft and its open questions.

    Check required sections and unsupported claims. Give the reviewer the evidence and decisions that need attention.

  4. KEEP THE DECISION WITH PEOPLEApprove before publishing.

    The accountable reviewer accepts or corrects the work. Only approved content moves to release.
