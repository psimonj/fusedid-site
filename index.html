Can this help if we already use AI?
Yes. You may already use AI for writing, research or individual tasks. FUSED adds a colleague with an ongoing understanding of your business and a team responsible for keeping it useful. We start with the tools you have.
What do we actually get first?
A named AI colleague your team can ask about products, processes and agreed decisions through Outlook and Teams. It researches the business, asks about gaps and remembers the decisions your team confirms. FUSED manages the setup, learning and support. You can use that starting service on its own and add skills when there is work you want it to take on.
Will we have to keep feeding it information?
It does the research, revisits agreed sources and asks about gaps. Your team supplies the context it cannot discover: private decisions, the reasons behind them and changes that need confirming. You keep it in the loop through email and Teams; FUSED manages the ongoing knowledge upkeep.
Do we need to know exactly what we want it to do?
No. A broad role or a part of the business that needs more capacity is enough to begin. We help shape a useful starting point. The colleague can also suggest skills as it learns where your team needs help.
Is it a person, or software?
It is AI, clearly identified as such. “Cloud colleague” describes a familiar way to work with it: a name, a role, email and conversations in Teams. FUSED provides the people responsible for designing, running and supporting the service.
Does everything have to run in Microsoft 365?
No. Outlook and Teams are familiar starting points, and we confirm the right identity, permissions and licensing for your environment. Other suitable business tools can be connected as the role and skills require. Explore how it fits your existing tools.
